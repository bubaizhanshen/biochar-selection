# Data Use

The MIT license applies to the software. Original scientific observations
remain subject to their source terms. The IBU/Sr/Cd response workbooks are not
included in this package. The separately documented As(V) table in
`data/asv/analysis_input.csv` contains screened and figure-digitized,
source-derived values; see [`data/asv/README.md`](data/asv/README.md) for source
attribution and the scope of this redistribution. No additional license is
granted for the original source material.

The other source annotations identify workbook rows, source-specific material
labels, reviewed operating conditions, and split membership. They do not
include the measured capacities or material-descriptor tables from the
IBU/Sr/Cd workbooks. Source identifiers and preparation details are provided so
users can acquire and cite the original records.

Preparation of the IBU/Sr/Cd analysis requires separately acquired SI
workbooks. The additional metal cases also require a user-supplied article XML
file. The scripts do not download subscription content or require publisher
credentials. The As(V) table is the only response table included here; its
source-specific limits and digitization procedure are described in its data
README. Neither reconstruction nor citation grants a new right to redistribute
other source data.
