# Data Use

The MIT license applies to the software. Original scientific observations remain
subject to their source terms and are not included in this package.

Source annotations identify workbook rows, source-specific material labels,
reviewed operating conditions, and split membership. They do not include measured
capacities or material-descriptor tables. Source identifiers and preparation
details are provided so users can acquire and cite the original records.

Preparation requires separately acquired SI workbooks. The additional metal
cases also require a user-supplied article XML file. The scripts do not download
subscription content or require publisher credentials. Generated inputs and
predictions contain source-derived observations; keep them local unless their
redistribution is permitted. Neither reconstruction nor citation grants a new
right to redistribute source data.
